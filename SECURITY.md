# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **dev@asend.now** with:

- a description of the issue and its impact
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- affected component, contract address, or app version

Please do not open a public issue or disclose the issue publicly until we have released a fix.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 2 business days
- An initial assessment within 7 days
- Credit in our release notes once fixed, if you want it

## Scope

- Asend mobile apps (iOS, Android)
- Asend backend APIs
- Smart contracts deployed by Asend on BNB Smart Chain
